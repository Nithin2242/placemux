For Task 24, I completed a launch rehearsal focused on locking metrics governance before release. The implementation establishes a governed KPI registry with explicit business definitions, owners, reviewers, source mappings, refresh SLAs, versions, criticality and certification states. The goal is to prevent launch reporting from depending on ambiguous or undocumented KPI definitions.

The launch framework classifies metrics as CERTIFIED, STALE, DEGRADED, BLOCKED or DEPRECATED. Critical KPIs are required to be certified before launch, while deprecated metrics are excluded from launch views. Each governed KPI must have complete ownership, source, version and refresh metadata, and changes to formulas or source logic require a version increment and review record.

The synthetic dry run contains 10 KPI definitions. Eight are certified, one is degraded, and one legacy KPI is deprecated and excluded from launch reporting. All 10 metrics have owners, reviewers, sources, versions and valid refresh SLAs. All critical KPIs are certified, so the launch readiness decision is PASS. The validation checks recorded zero duplicate metric IDs, zero duplicate metric names, zero invalid status values, zero missing owners or reviewers, zero missing source/version metadata, zero invalid refresh SLAs, and zero critical KPIs left uncertified.

The result is a reproducible launch governance layer rather than a claim about production PlaceMux performance. The repository artifacts provide the metric governance rules, registry, validation script, generated validation output and an executive-ready launch dashboard. Together they create a controlled hand-off from analytical definitions to launch KPIs and make future metric changes auditable through versioning and review.
