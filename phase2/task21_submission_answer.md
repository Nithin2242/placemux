For Task 21, I applied compliance-aware reporting governance for the PlaceMux analytics layer. The framework defines how reporting should be classified, validated, access-controlled, versioned and monitored so that dashboards remain trustworthy and traceable as the platform scales.

The governance model introduces clear reporting classes for executive KPIs, operational dashboards, analytical datasets, sensitive detail and external or shared reports. It standardizes the metadata required for a governed metric, including its business definition, formula, grain, source, transformation logic, owner, reviewer, refresh cadence, freshness requirement, version and known limitations. This creates a clear lineage from source events or tables through the validated layer to the final report.

I defined explicit data-quality gates for completeness, uniqueness, referential integrity, valid values, temporal integrity, freshness and reconciliation. Reporting states are standardized as CERTIFIED, STALE, DEGRADED and BLOCKED so that failed controls are visible rather than silently producing misleading numbers. Role-based access was also defined for students, colleges/TPOs, recruiters, finance and operations, analytics, and administrators, with a least-privilege and data-minimization approach.

Because the task is a governance and foundations task and the brief does not provide a production compliance dataset or audit environment, I have not fabricated production compliance results or a legal certification. I also added DPDP-aware purpose and consent controls, including versioned purpose/consent metadata and rules for handling withdrawal or permission changes, together with security foundations such as least-privilege access, protected storage, audit logging and controlled exports.

The completed deliverables are `phase2/reporting_governance.md`, `phase2/compliance_reporting_plan.md`, `phase2/reporting_metric_catalog.md`, `phase2/reporting_governance_controls.csv`, `phase2/reporting_governance_demo.py`, and `phase2/reporting_governance_validation.json`. The control-catalog dry run passed with all 12 governance controls marked CHECKED. This validates the documentation/control catalog itself; it is not a production legal, privacy or security certification.
