Data-Subject Controls

  • Consent is required before experimentation exposure.
  • Deletion requests exclude the subject before exposure and analysis.
  • Experiment withdrawal is honored before exposure and downstream measurement.
  • Use opaque subject identifiers; do not export direct identifiers into analytics outputs.
  • Version consent and purpose metadata so experiment eligibility can be audited.
  • Production deletion workflows must propagate to derived experiment datasets according to governed retention rules.

Resilience Checks

  • Stable assignment from immutable subject ID hash.
  • No exposure without eligibility.
  • No invalid variant values.
  • No duplicate subject identifiers.
  • Balanced control/treatment allocation within tolerance.
  • Primary analysis rows exist for both arms.
  • Machine-readable validation output retained alongside the experiment results.
