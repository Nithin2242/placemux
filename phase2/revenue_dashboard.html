Source & Definitions

Primary revenue source: phase2/payment_events_demo.csv

Application conversion source: phase2/pay_per_application_events_demo.csv

Customer definition: company_id for the payment/revenue layer.

ARPU: Recognized revenue divided by distinct paying companies.

Cohort: Calendar month of the company's first successful payment.

Reproducible command: python phase2/revenue_dashboard_demo.py

The dashboard combines separate synthetic demonstration streams. Revenue from those streams is intentionally not summed together. Production reporting should replace them with a governed, authoritative event and financial data source.
