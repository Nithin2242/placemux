# College-Value Dashboard Metrics

## Purpose
Measure the value delivered to colleges through student readiness, application activity, placement outcomes and company engagement.

## Core metrics
- Registered Students = distinct students registered in the reporting population.
- Eligible Students = students satisfying configured placement eligibility rules.
- Profile Completion Rate = students with complete profiles / registered students x 100.
- Students with Applications = distinct eligible students with at least one application.
- Placement Rate = distinct placed students / eligible students x 100.
- Average Package = mean package LPA among placed students with a recorded package.
- Median Package = median package LPA among placed students with a recorded package.
- Company Engagement = distinct companies with at least one engagement for the college.
- Successful Outcomes = company engagement records reaching a successful outcome.

## Interpretation
The dashboard is intended to help a college identify readiness gaps, funnel leakage, placement performance and recruiter demand. College-to-college comparisons should be interpreted with branch mix, eligibility rules, recruiter mix and time-window context.
