Recommendation

Action

Mandate that lifeboat access and evacuation drills be proportional across all cabin classes in future maritime safety policy — not concentrated near upper-deck, higher-fare cabins.

Expected impact

Raising 3rd-class survival to the 2nd-class rate (50.9%) would translate to an estimated 70+ additional survivors in this dataset alone — the scale of loss driven by access, not chance.
