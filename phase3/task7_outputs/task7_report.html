Optimization Hypotheses

  • Single-purchase customers are not sufficiently re-engaged.
  • High-value customers retain better than low-value customers.
  • Customers with larger first orders convert more frequently.

Business Recommendations

  • Improve onboarding immediately after first purchase.
  • Launch loyalty and retention campaigns.
  • Track second-purchase conversion as a primary KPI.
  • Run targeted upsell experiments.
  • Create high-value customer retention programs.
